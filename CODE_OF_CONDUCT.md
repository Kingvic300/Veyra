# Code of conduct

Veyra is built by and for people across many countries and backgrounds. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not people.
- Respect differing experience levels and first languages.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers by email (see the contact page on the Veyra website). Maintainers may remove content and suspend or ban participants who break these rules. Reports are handled confidentially.
